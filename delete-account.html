Delete My Account

We believe deleting your data should be as easy as signing up for it. To request deletion of your account and personal information, just email us:

Email hello@accessalertsapp.com

When you email us, please include the email address associated with your Access Alerts account so we can find and remove it. We'll confirm once it's done.

Deleting your account will remove:

  • Your account and login information
  • Your watchlist and price/volume/news alert settings
  • Your paper trading history

Note: if you have an active paid subscription, deleting your account does not automatically issue a refund for the current billing period — see our Terms of Service for details.
