Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Access Alerts ("we," "us," "our") provides stock price, volume, and news alerts along with informational market data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We built this app to help people stay informed about the markets — not to profit from your data, and not to trade on your behalf.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you give us directly, such as your email address when you join our waitlist, create an account, or contact us.
  • Account and preference data, such as your watchlist, alert settings, and subscription plan, once accounts are live.
  • Usage information, such as which features you use, collected to improve the app.
  • Payment information is handled by our payment processor directly — we do not store your full card details on our own servers.

What We Don't Do

Access Alerts is an informational and alerting tool. We do not execute trades, hold funds, or have access to your brokerage or bank account. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

How We Use Information

  • To deliver the alerts and features you sign up for
  • To communicate with you about your account or our service
  • To improve and maintain the app
  • To comply with legal obligations

Market Data

Price, volume, and news data shown in the app is sourced from third-party market data providers. That data is provided for informational purposes and may be delayed or subject to the terms of our data providers.

Your Choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing hello@accessalertsapp.com. See our Delete My Account page for details.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including keeping sensitive credentials such as API keys out of our public codebase and restricting access to production systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Access Alerts is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Access Alerts grows. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@accessalertsapp.com.

This policy is a good-faith starting point for an early-stage app and will be reviewed by legal counsel before Access Alerts processes real customer payments at scale. It is not a substitute for legal advice.
